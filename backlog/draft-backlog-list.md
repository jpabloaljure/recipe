| D-12 | **Our unmute button follows YouTube's own sound control, and sound carries over to the next videos in Chrome:** hide the button while the active video has sound and show it whenever it is muted, also when the home cook uses YouTube's own mute or volume control; and make the choice hold for the following videos | REC-003 real-device tests (owner's Chrome and Android, 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24) | Built in five steps and **rolled back on 2026-11-24** to the REC-003 state of commit `b32acf5`; the code, tests and spec text are kept under the git tag `archive/rec-003-follow-player-sound` (commits `72af07b` to `d79f526`). Why it is not in the MVP code: on the owner's Chrome the button and the sound went out of step (the video looked unmuted but silent), and after an unmute the next videos stalled, silent, on the player's loading circle, for 3 s to 21 s. The logs show Chrome **accepting** an unmute (`isMuted()` false, no error, no "autoplay blocked" event) and then leaving the player buffering; muting the video resumed it within 0.1 s; a tap on our own button did not prevent it. Likely cause, not confirmed: Chrome wants a click inside each video's own iframe, and every swipe creates a new iframe, so sound cannot carry over with one player per slide. Options: **A** keep the attempt with a stall watchdog (works with a short hiccup after a tap); **B** no automatic sound (needs the REC-003 criterion and PRD §5.2 changed and validated); **C** one reused player that keeps its permission (a large change that conflicts with one iframe per slide, belongs with REC-004; spike first). Next: spike C with `yt-check.html`, then decide. REC-003 itself keeps the one-way button and the start-muted-then-unmute flow. Evidence: `backlog/evidence/D-12-follow-the-players-sound/` (logs transcribed, screenshots, real-Chrome measurements, check page, options and resume steps). |
